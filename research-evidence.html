Appendix C. Research evidence

C1. Selected outputs and my contribution

These are some outputs selected for the research case. For each, I give the reference, the venue and its rank, my role, the question it addresses, what I contributed myself, and the result and recognition. Where I am not the first author, I state the roles of my students, my postdoctoral researcher and the co-authors.

J20. Appendix A6.2

Venue and rank: Pattern Recognition, [Q1, IF 8.8].

My role: first author

Question and importance: How the structure kept in a data representation affects clustering. [one more sentence]

My contribution: Idea initiation, Model conception, Experimentation. The co-authors, criticism, result validation and proofread the paper

Result and recognition: Best Paper Award, IEEE Oman Section. Appendix C3.2.

C10. Appendix A6.5

Venue and rank: Canadian AI 2025, CORE B

My role: supervisor of the first author (MSc student); M. Allaoui, my postdoctoral researcher, is a co-author

Question and importance: Learning representations across tasks, not for one task only.

My contribution: Supervision. Ask the research question. The other co-authors address it and develop the method.

Result and recognition: The first meta unsupervised algorithm published. Best Conference Student Paper Award. Appendix D2.

J10 and J09. Appendix A6.2.

Venue and rank: Expert Systems with Applications [Q1, IF 9.4]; Knowledge-Based Systems [Q1, IF 8.0].

My role: third author of J10 and second author of J09; the first author of both is my postdoctoral researcher, M. Allaoui

Question and importance: How to improve low-dimensional embeddings used for visualization.

My contribution: Supervision. Improve the formal model, validate experiments and proofread the paper supervision. The 1st author initiates the idea and develop it, the author co-authors contribute in all steps.

Result and recognition: Good number of citations.

J26. Appendix A6.2.

Venue and rank: IEEE JSTARS [Q1, IF 6.3].

My role: first author.

Question and importance: Detecting meaningful change in satellite images when no labelled examples exist.

My contribution: Initiation and end-to-end development of the idea. The other co-author contributed to all steps.

J01 and J08. Appendix A6.2.

Venue and rank: Sensors [Q1, IF 4]; Signals [Q1, IF 2.9].

My role: last author and supervisor; the first author is my PhD student; the 3rd co-author is a co-supervisor.

Question and importance: Detecting sleep apnea and classifying sleep stages from physiological signals.

My contribution: Help in designing a valid prediction model.

Result and recognition: Achieved state of the art in sleep stage and disorder detection.

C03 and C06. Appendix A6.5.

Venue and rank: UAI’26, CORE A*; AISTATS’26, CORE A*

My role: Supervisor and second author of both; the first author of both is my postdoctoral researcher.

Question and importance: reliable anomaly decisions, and efficient graph neural networks for classification.

My contribution: Help in refining the problem formulation and validating experiments.

Result and recognition: Achieved the state of the art on many benchmark datasets.

Earlier work (J34, J35, J36, Appendix A6.2)

IEEE TGRS 2016 (J34), IEEE TIP 2015 (J35) and Pattern Recognition 2013 (J36) show the early direction of my research on image representation, change detection and historical documents, and are the source of the shared resources in Appendix C3. My role: first author of most of them.
