Leadership & service

Contributing to the university and the wider discipline.

5. Leadership and academic service

5.1 Institutional leadership

5.1.1 Service at Bishop's University

My service at Bishop's includes 1) Interim Chair in Fall 2024, 2) representation for Natural Sciences and Mathematics on the Senate Teaching and Learning Centre Initiative Committee, and 3) membership of the Task Force on Generative AI from 2024. These roles connect departmental operation with questions about teaching and the use of emerging technology in the University (Appendix A12).

5.1.2 Building research connections

Since January 2024, I have been Head of the AI Track at AMI-Lab (Research Centre on Aging, Université de Sherbrooke). I have also held adjunct professor appointments at UQO since January 2026 and at the Université de Sherbrooke since June 2026. These roles link my AI expertise with research on aging and related applications, and with graduate training outside Bishop’s. The joint publications and supervision in Sections 3 and 4 come from these relationships. I am a member and co-founder of BRAIN (Bishop’s Centre for Research in AI & Innovation), which is under construction.

5.1.3 Earlier leadership and institutional experience

At Sultan Qaboos University, I chaired the Course Files Committee and the Publicity Committee, served on committees relating to quality assurance, postgraduate studies, student advising, programming, and technical staff training, and held brief Interim Chair responsibilities. I also chaired two research groups (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, and Data Science and its Applications) in the Department of Computer Science. These roles provide background to my capacity to contribute to departmental and institutional work at Bishop's.

5.1.4 Evidence of contribution

As a member of the Task Force on Generative AI (since 2024), I initiated the idea of surveying Bishop’s members (librarian, IT, faculty and students) on how they use generative AI in teaching and IT service. I played an important role in the statistical analysis of the survey. Its results helped the Task Force to draw up guidelines for the use of generative AI at Bishop’s. The Task Force built on the survey in several rounds of meetings and discussions. As a result, generative AI has become one of the main sessions of the Bishop’s Academic Year Launch, which is held at the beginning of September each year. In September 2025, I co-organized the session “Artificial Intelligence and Bishop’s Mission, Vision and Values: Can they co-exist?”. See Appendix E1.

5.2 Professional service and knowledge exchange

5.2.1 Scholarly assessment and disciplinary service

My disciplinary service includes an associate editorship at the Journal on Image and Video Processing, participation in an NSERC evaluation committee for DG application Program evaluation, and membership of the “Conseil scientifique de Calcul Québec” from 2024. I also review regularly for journals and conferences in image processing, pattern recognition, machine learning, and remote sensing, including Pattern Recognition, IEEE Transactions on Image Processing, Neurocomputing, and Digital Signal Processing. I was named an Outstanding Reviewer at the British Machine Vision Conference (BMVC) in 2024 (Appendix E2). I served as an external reviewer of the MSc program in Computer Science at the University of Ouargla, Algeria (2022), for which the department issued a certificate (Appendix E1.2).

I am a member of the Canadian Artificial Intelligence Association (CAIAC) since 2025 and of the IEEE Computer Society since 2014, and I have been a member of the IEEE Geoscience and Remote Sensing Society, since 2019, where I also co-founded the Oman Section Chapter 29, Region8. See Appendix E2.6.

5.2.2 Conferences and academic communities

My conference service includes chairing the Vision and Multimodal Systems session at Canadian AI 2025 and serving as Awards Chair for M2GARSS 2024. Earlier contributions include further session chair roles at M2GARSS (2020, 2022), organization of the first international forum on remote sensing and machine learning in Oman (2018), I coordinate the multispectral document analysis contest reported at ICDAR 2015. I am also a co-founder of the IEEE GRSS Chapter in Oman. See Appendix E2. In 2024, I chaired the organizing committee of the first edition of the LINKS workshop (Linking Innovation, Networking and Knowledge Sharing) at CERIST, Algeria (Appendix E2).

5.2.3 Outreach and recruitment

At Bishop's, my outreach includes participation in the Rooted in the Townships Career Fair (April 2025, Appendix E3.1), a recruitment visits to Collège de Bois-de-Boulogne (February 2025), and departmental ambassador activities at universities in Algeria (December 2025). These activities bring academic expertise to prospective students and external communities; any subsequent enrolment or partnership outcomes are documented separately rather than assumed.

5.2.4 Professional consultation and public communication

My consultation experience includes work with PreScouter (USA), DeepMinds (Appendix A12, E3.2) an analysis of electricity-consumption anomalies for Mazoon Electricity Company (Oman), and a collaboration between SQU and Clarivate on the AI technology landscape. This work connects research expertise with questions raised by organizations outside the University, described here in non-confidential terms. Earlier public communication includes a documentary appearance on multispectral analysis of historical manuscripts and coverage of the 2018 Oman remote-sensing forum, retained as career context and dated accordingly.
