Application letter

The application for promotion to Full Professor.

Application letter

October 29, 2026

Chair of the Evaluation Committee

Bishop's University

Dear Members of the Evaluation Committee,

Please find enclosed my application for promotion from Associate Professor to Full Professor in the Department of Computer Science. I was promoted to Associate Professor at Bishop’s in July 2026, and I submit this application at the Committee’s invitation, for the current cycle. Before joining Bishop’s, I served for six years as Assistant and then Associate Professor at Sultan Qaboos University (SQU), Oman (2017–2023). In accordance with Article 7.06 g) of the Faculty Collective Agreement, I respectfully ask that my research, teaching and service from those six years be included in the assessment. The supporting evidence is attached in the appendices.

Teaching is central to my work. At Bishop's I have taught and developed courses spanning programming, databases, artificial intelligence, neural networks, data visualization, and natural language processing, at both the undergraduate and graduate levels. The 2026 Excellence in Teaching Award at Bishop's (Appendix B4) recognizes this work; my earlier teaching at SQU included full curriculum development for a new MSc in Data Science and recognition as Distinguished Academician (Best Teacher), College of Science, for 2020–2021 (Appendix B4).

My research asks how useful, interpretable structure can be learned from complex data, particularly when labels are scarce. It connects matrix factorization, manifold learning, and meta-unsupervised learning with applications in medical data analysis, remote sensing, and most recently privacy-preserving federated learning. My 38 journal articles and 36 refereed conference papers include work in Pattern Recognition, IEEE Transactions on Image Processing, IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing, Knowledge-Based Systems, and Expert Systems with Applications. This program is currently supported by an NSERC Discovery Development Grant, a Mitacs Accelerate award, a CFI John R. Evans Leaders Fund award as co-PI, and three Bishop's Research and Creative Activity Grants.

Graduate and undergraduate supervision connects this research to student development. My record includes postdoctoral, doctoral, master’s and undergraduate supervision across institutions in Canada, Oman, Algeria and Qatar. My students have won two Best Conference Student Paper Awards in 2025 at Bishop’s, and at Sultan Qaboos University a Best Supervised MSc Thesis award (2020) and two Best Published Paper awards (2023 and 2024). My role as Head of the AI Track at AMI-Lab and my 2026 adjunct appointments at the Université de Sherbrooke and UQO extend the collaborative environment available for this training.

I have also contributed through departmental leadership, University committees, scholarly assessment, conference organization, and outreach. At Bishop’s, this includes serving as Interim Chair in Fall 2024, membership of the Task Force on Generative AI, and representation on the Senate Teaching and Learning Centre Initiative Committee. At Sultan Qaboos University (2017–2023), I chaired the Course Files Committee, the Publicity Committee and two research groups (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning; Data Science and its Applications), served on the quality assurance and postgraduate studies committees, and held brief Interim Chair responsibilities.

The enclosed narrative, CV, and evidence register set out the basis for my request. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss this dossier and my plans for continued contribution to Bishop's University.

Sincerely,

Rachid Hedjam, PhD

Associate Professor, Department of Computer Science
